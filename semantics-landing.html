The Problem

Billions of fans.
Lost in translation.

The global music industry generates $28B annually, yet the vast majority of its catalog is locked in English — inaccessible to billions of fans who don't speak the language.

01

Lost Emotion

Word-for-word translations strip away the slang, rhythm, and cultural nuance that make a song resonate. The meaning arrives. The feeling doesn't.

02

Missed Markets

Artists leave billions on the table by not reaching Spanish, Arabic, Hindi, and Mandarin-speaking fans who would love their music — if they could understand it.

03

No Voice Fidelity

Existing translation tools treat lyrics like documents. They don't know that Tupac speaks differently than Taylor Swift — and that difference is everything.

04

Cultural Disconnect

Music is the most universal human language, yet the words that carry its stories remain walled off by language barriers that technology has long had the power to solve.

How It Works

Translation that
feels like the artist.

01

Artist Voice Analysis

Semantics doesn't just translate words — it first understands the artist. Their cadence, slang, cultural references, emotional register, and lyrical style are analyzed before a single word is translated.

Proprietary AI Model
02

Culturally-Adaptive Translation

Our engine maps English slang, idioms, and street poetry to equivalent expressions in the target language — finding the phrases that land with the same weight and attitude in that culture.

50+ Languages
03

Voice-Cloned Audio Delivery

Using advanced voice synthesis, the translated lyrics are delivered in a voice that mirrors the artist's tone, timbre, and delivery — so fans hear their favorite artist, not a robot reading a script.

ElevenLabs Voice Cloning
04

Platform Integration

Delivered via API to streaming platforms, artist websites, and music apps — so the experience lives where fans already are, not in a separate app they have to find.

🇫🇷 Translated — French
Je suis descendu à l'infirmerie Saint-James
J'ai vu ma belle là-bas
Étendue sur une longue table blanche
Si froide, si douce, si belle

Laisse-la partir, que Dieu la bénisse
Où qu'elle aille sous ce ciel
Elle peut chercher partout dans ce monde
Jamais elle trouvera un homme comme moi
🎤 Artist Voice Note: Armstrong's weary tenderness and dignified grief are preserved through formal yet warm French phrasing. The blues cadence is maintained through parallel structure and the bittersweet final line retains its aching pride.
